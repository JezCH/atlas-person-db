# ATLAS historical Person identity

- Resolve actual Person UUIDs and evidence before deciding whether a candidate is new. An exported spreadsheet or old audit cannot prove current Production absence.
- Distinguish personal name, regnal title, epithet, dynasty, polity, place, fictional character, legendary figure, deity and non-person group.
- Use era, region, office, and attested source to disambiguate homonyms and transliterations; identical surface names neither prove a duplicate nor justify merging identities.
- Preserve distinct rulership/activity periods as separate Activities attached to one proven Person.
- HOLD uncertain candidates with specific missing evidence; do not fabricate dates, activity, state membership or registration status.
- Keep first-class Source links and reviewed Authoring request contracts. Destructive identity changes require explicit approval.
