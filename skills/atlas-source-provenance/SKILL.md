# ATLAS historical provenance

- First specify the individual assertion (identity, date, place, office, polity, importance). Different claims need separate supporting evidence.
- Distinguish original primary material, critical edition, scholarly synthesis, transcription, and unsourced retelling. A video title/name hit alone is not identity confirmation.
- Preserve independent Source object, original source/video identifiers, author/date and precise evidence location without inventing missing citations.
- Keep disputed claims and differing date ranges as separate sourced assertions rather than averaging or erasing contradictions.
- Deduplicate investigations by original source ID where applicable; spelling-only matching cannot prove uniqueness.
- AI candidate research is non-authoritative; only approved reviewed Authoring changes may become canonical, with existing writer and runtime gates.
