## 2. Constitutional primary source — same Turkish state, different government phase

Official Türkiye Constitutional Court reproduces **1921 Teşkilât-ı Esasiye Kanunu** as originally adopted **20 January 1921**, and amendments of **29 October 1923 (Law No. 364)**. Original Art. 1 vested sovereignty in the nation, Art. 2 legislative/executive authority in the Great National Assembly, and **Art. 3** declared that **Türkiye Devleti** is governed by the Grand National Assembly and its government is called the **Government of the Grand National Assembly**. Amended **Art. 1** inserted **'Türkiye Devletinin şekli Hükümeti, Cumhuriyettir'** (state's form of government is a republic).

**Thus 1923 October 29 was an amendment to the preexisting national state order, not the destruction of one distinct sovereign state followed by creation of a separate external sovereign**. The same assembly adopted republican government, with the offices of President and responsible Cabinet replacing the assembly-government executive arrangement. Official TBMM History confirms April 23, 1920 opening, 1921 constitution, November 1, 1922 abolition of the Ottoman Sultanate, October 29, 1923 proclamation of the Republic and appointment of the first President, and the later 1924 constitution. National Education Ministry confirms substantive continuity while distinguishing the provisional assembly-government's cabinet appointment procedure from the new republican executive offices.

**Important sovereignty nuance:** Ankara's revolutionary government coexisted in contest with the *Ottoman Sultan/Istanbul Government* during the war/occupation. That was a genuine competing authority within the broader Ottoman imperial collapse, not evidence that the Ankara GNAT and the **post-1923** Republic should be treated as two simultaneous successor nations. Neither should the entire Ottoman state retroactively be labeled Turkey in 1920.
