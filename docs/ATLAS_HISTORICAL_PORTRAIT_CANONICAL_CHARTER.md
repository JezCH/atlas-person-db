# ATLAS Historical Portrait Canonical Charter v1.0

Status: **CANONICAL / REQUIRED**

This is the authoritative production contract for every ATLAS Person portrait. Every new chat, worker, image-generation session, reviewer and future automation MUST apply it before creating or approving a portrait.

## 1. Mission
ATLAS does **not** imitate an old picture. It reconstructs the **real human being who lived**. Use every reasonably obtainable appearance source, remove distortions introduced by artistic convention/medium, preserve genuine individual traits, and reconstruct missing information only inside historically plausible bounds.

Core question: **If this person were alive in front of us, what would they most plausibly look like?** A reconstruction is not proof of uncertain facial details.

## 2. Non-negotiable priority
When objectives conflict:
1. historical/source accuracy
2. individual recognizability and attested traits
3. human physical realism and three-dimensional presence
4. historical dignity, monumentality and glory
5. aesthetic beauty

Beauty, drama, symmetry, heroism or model preference may NEVER overwrite stronger evidence.

## 3. Research before generation
Never begin from a generic prompt. Seek and compare as many relevant sources as reasonably available:
- photographs/film/direct physical records/death masks
- authenticated or strongly accepted life portraits
- contemporary paintings, drawings, miniatures, sketches
- contemporary sculpture, busts, statues, reliefs
- coins, medals, seals and contemporary iconography
- contemporary/near-contemporary written physical descriptions
- records of age, scars, disability, hair, beard, complexion, build and distinguishing traits
- archaeological/anthropological evidence legitimately connected to the person
- period-correct clothing, uniform, armour, headwear, jewellery, insignia and grooming evidence
- reliable later copies of lost originals
- scholarly facial reconstructions
- later reconstructions/traditional portraits/conjectural images at lower weight
- historical population context for genuinely unknown physical details

Do not stop at the most famous image when independent evidence exists.

## 4. Evidence hierarchy
Evidence is weighted, not mechanically counted.

**A — direct/high-authority:** reliable photograph/direct physical record; authenticated life portrait with strong likeness value; a visual source widely accepted by relevant scholarship as a strong likeness. Preserve closely. AI translates rendering, not the face.

**B — multiple contemporary likeness sources:** independent contemporary portraits/busts/coins/drawings/descriptions converging on traits. Reconstruct shared signals.

**C — partial contemporary evidence:** stylized coin/sculpture/relief/schematic portrait/textual description/damaged likeness. Extract defensible traits cautiously.

**D — contextual reconstruction:** no dependable individual likeness. Period, geography, supportable ancestry/population history, sex, age, status, lifestyle, clothing and grooming constrain a plausible reconstruction.

**E — later conjecture:** later portraits, traditional iconography, popular/scholarly reconstructions and artistic conjectures inform but never override stronger evidence.

**F — symbolic:** legendary/mythic or exceptionally unsupported appearance. Never present a freely invented face as authenticated.

One strong direct source can outweigh many derivative weak images. Copies from one prototype are not independent corroboration.

## 5. Reconstruct the human, not the medium
Historical media encode information **and distortion**. Separate them.

Do not reproduce as anatomy: traditional-art flatness; iconographic frontality/profile convention; schematic proportions; marble/bronze/coin material; court/religious/imperial idealization; perspective limitations; conventional beauty/status formulas.

Preserve when supported: recurring craniofacial proportions; face width/length; cheekbone/jaw/chin; nose; eye spacing/brow; hairline/hair/beard; age; scars/injuries; credible asymmetry/unusual traits.

A flat traditional painted face is evidence about the sitter, not an instruction to make a flat human. A marble bust is anatomical evidence, not marble skin.

## 6. Missing-face reconstruction
Less direct evidence requires **more disciplined inference**, not more creative freedom. Use defensible context to define a range of plausible appearance: origin; supportable ancestry/population history; period demography; age/sex; documented family evidence; health/lifestyle/social environment; grooming; material culture.

Modern racial caricatures, national-face stereotypes, phrenology and simplistic "ethnic face" templates are prohibited. Population context constrains possibilities; it does not prove an individual's exact face. Lower evidence means lower confidence.

## 7. Civilization-neutral rendering
ATLAS uses one common rendering language. Chinese subjects are not rendered anatomically flat because of ink-painting convention; medieval Europeans are not manuscript-miniature humans; Egyptians are not tomb-painting anatomy.

Culture determines historically correct appearance, clothing, grooming, objects and material culture—not optical distortions of its historical art medium.

## 8. Canonical visual language
**historical realism + Neoclassical discipline + restrained Romantic grandeur**

These are rendering/composition/emotional language, NOT the subject's culture.

**Realism:** living anatomy; natural skin/hair/eyes/age; believable materials; coherent light/3D form; imperfections/asymmetry; no beauty-filter aesthetics.

**Neoclassical discipline:** stability, clarity, dignity, controlled gesture, monumentality, person-centered hierarchy.

**Restrained Romanticism:** luminous depth, emotional gravity, historical presence, selective dramatic light, glory without fantasy spectacle.

Jacques-Louis David's *The Coronation of Napoleon* is an **expressive reference** for material specificity, historical grandeur, controlled composition and luminous importance—not a costume, ethnicity, facial, palette or composition template.

Result: neither modern cosplay photography nor an antique-painting replica, but a highly realistic painterly portrait of a living historical human.

## 9. Dignity and glory
Create dignity through posture, gaze, controlled light, tonal depth, material fidelity, calm confidence, focus and restrained atmospheric grandeur.

Never create it by beautifying the face, changing attested anatomy, unjustifiably enlarging physique, altering phenotype, removing documented age/traits, automatic crowns/weapons, fantasy glow, battle spectacle or propaganda poses.

**Grandeur is allowed; cheap grandiosity is not.**

## 10. Canonical composition
- **4:5 vertical**
- canonical upload ceiling **1024×1280**
- chest-up/bust
- head/shoulders readable
- stable centered visual mass
- default: **slight three-quarter view from the subject's left-diagonal/front orientation toward the viewer**
- enough turn to reveal nose, cheekbone, jaw and cranial depth
- living eyes generally toward/near viewer
- calm dignified expression
- natural posture
- no extreme foreshortening/action pose

3/4 is a default, not a reason to damage a high-authority likeness or mechanically mirror source anatomy.

## 11. Age
Use the most defensible age: strongest likeness age when recognition depends on it; otherwise principal historical period where evidence permits; otherwise a documented mature age. Never automatically rejuvenate/de-age for grandeur.

## 12. Clothing, armour, regalia, grooming
Treat every visible item as an evidence problem. Verify garment type/cut, fabric, known colour, armour type/date, uniform regulations, insignia, headwear, jewellery, regalia, hairstyle, facial hair, appropriate cosmetics and status accessories.

Use exact documented items when available; otherwise the narrowest period/place/status-appropriate reconstruction. No generic ancient robes, generic medieval armour, fantasy armour, costume-shop styling, anachronistic medals, wrong dynastic crowns or century mixing.

## 13. Props
Person first. Props only when historically tied to the individual and useful. No automatic book/sword/crown/compass/religious object by occupation. Minimal props.

## 14. Background/UI separation
Generated portrait = **person layer**. ATLAS UI owns classification/background presentation.

Do not bake in era frames, UI borders, medallions, badges, labels, names/text, representative-domain colours or elaborate narrative scenery. Any unavoidable internal background stays quiet/subordinate.

Legacy proposals saying frame=era, era-frame skins or frame medallions are **superseded** for portrait-image production.

## 15. Lighting/colour
Controlled gallery/historical-portrait lighting: enough direction to model facial planes; softness for skin/textile detail; coherent source; restrained highlights; deep readable shadows. No crushed-black gimmick, neon grading or glamour-studio look.

Colour is rich/material/archival without universal brown/desaturation/fake aging. Skin tone follows evidence/plausible biology, not a source painting palette.

## 16. Physical realism
Living skin variation without grotesque hyperdetail; realistic eyes; natural hair masses/implantation; believable facial hair; coherent ears/neck/shoulders/hands; natural asymmetry; correct age texture. No plastic skin, over-sharpened pseudo-photo or uncanny symmetry.

## 17. Sex, ancestry, phenotype
Use only evidence and plausible historical context. Do not modernize toward beauty ideals, exaggerate ethnic traits, or homogenize into a generic global face. Mixed/uncertain populations require uncertainty, not stereotypes.

## 18. Special evidence cases
**Reliable photo:** preserve identity extremely closely; translate rendering, not anatomy.

**Detailed trusted pre-photographic portrait:** preserve facial geometry/peculiarities; restore 3D anatomy, living skin and coherent light while cautiously correcting medium/style distortion.

**Sculpture/bust:** use multiple views when available; reconstruct soft tissue conservatively; idealization is not automatically literal.

**Coin/medal:** profile geometry/repeated traits are evidence; do not invent fine frontal detail with false confidence.

**Text-only:** use clear described traits exactly; fill the rest conservatively.

**Famous later canonical image:** secondary evidence; determine whether it derives from lost original, scholarship, ideology or pure imagination before weighting.

**Contradictory sources:** rank provenance, date, independence, purpose and idealization; never blindly average.

## 19. Legendary/mythic/uncertain historicity
Do not turn mythology into forensic history. Use grounded material culture/canonical iconography where relevant; mark symbolic/speculative metadata; never imply authenticated likeness.

## 20. Modern/recent persons
Reliable photos leave little justification for facial invention. Preserve identity. Painterly treatment must not materially alter facial structure. Apply rights/provenance/project policy; never call generated reconstruction an original historical photograph.

## 21. Provenance/confidence
Every portrait should preserve: facial sources; clothing/grooming sources; direct/contemporary/later status; inferred elements; confidence; approved generation/revision.

Semantic states: verified/archival where applicable, grounded_reconstruction, speculative, symbolic.
Source bases: photo, painting/drawing, sculpture/relief, coin/medal, textual_description, archaeological/contextual evidence, none.
Confidence: high/medium/low.
Source roles distinguish facial_reference, clothing_reference, iconography_reference, textual_description, context_reference.

Photorealistic rendering does NOT upgrade evidentiary confidence.

## 22. Required dossier before generation
1. identity/dates
2. target age/period
3. strongest facial evidence
4. independent corroboration
5. contradictions/likely artistic distortions
6. written physical descriptions
7. ancestry/population context only where needed
8. hair/facial-hair evidence
9. clothing/headwear/regalia evidence
10. distinctive physical traits
11. confidence by feature
12. reconstruction gaps
13. elements that MUST NOT be invented
14. final visual instructions

For important/ambiguous figures, evaluate contradictions before generation.

## 23. Prompt architecture
Order:
1. identity
2. evidence-locked facial traits
3. uncertain/reconstructed traits
4. age/condition
5. correct hair/grooming
6. correct clothing/regalia
7. canonical 4:5 chest-up 3/4 composition
8. realism + Neoclassical discipline + restrained Romantic grandeur
9. light/material treatment
10. negative constraints
11. uncertainty discipline

Evidence-locked identity comes before generic style words so style cannot overwrite identity.

## 24. Universal negative constraints
Unless evidence justifies exception: no fantasy RPG/card art; anime/cartoon; generic game concept art; Hollywood casting substitution; fashion/glamour photography; beauty filters; excessive symmetry; youthification; sexualization; bodybuilder heroification; exaggerated emotion; battle pose/shouting; excessive cinematic effects; fantasy equipment; anachronisms; culturally wrong garments; modern hair/makeup; racial stereotype; source-art flatness as anatomy; marble/bronze as skin; fake sepia/aging; elaborate scenery; text/signatures/captions/badges/frames; invented heraldry; automatic occupational props.

## 25. Review gate
Beauty is not approval.

**Identity/evidence:** strongest likeness preserved? weak famous image overriding strong source? distinctive traits preserved?

**Reconstruction:** unsupported details plausible rather than stereotyped? medium distortions corrected? uncertainty proportional?

**Historical material:** clothing, armour, headwear, insignia, jewellery, grooming, age correct?

**Human realism:** 3D anatomy, skin/hair/eyes, age, asymmetry, body plausible?

**ATLAS consistency:** 4:5; chest-up; default left-diagonal/front 3/4; stable composition; canonical visual language; dignified historical presence?

**Contamination:** reject anachronism, fantasy, cultural-art-style anatomical distortion, glamour beautification, UI/frame/text or unjustified props.

If a historically more accurate result is less conventionally beautiful, choose the historically more accurate result.

## 26. Revision rule
New evidence triggers evidence-hierarchy reassessment and revision/regeneration when material. Preserve provenance/revision history. Portraits are evidence-responsive reconstructions, not immutable artworks.

## 27. Cross-chat/worker consistency
Every portrait task inherits this charter. No worker may replace it with a preferred art style, source-culture art style, generic portrait template, famous weak later depiction, prettier face or previous ATLAS portrait. Each person starts from evidence; rendering language/composition remain consistent.

## 28. Canonical short instruction
> ATLAS reconstructs the historical human, not the historical artwork. Research and rank all reasonably obtainable direct and indirect evidence; preserve strongly attested individual traits; remove distortions caused by medium and cultural art convention; and fill only genuinely unknown features within historically defensible bounds using period, geographic, population, material-culture and textual context without stereotyping. Render the result as a 4:5 chest-up living-human portrait, normally in a slight left-diagonal/front three-quarter view, using highly realistic anatomy and materials with Neoclassical compositional dignity and restrained Romantic light and grandeur. Historical accuracy and identity always outrank beauty or drama. Do not invent frames, text, fantasy, glamour, anachronisms or unsupported symbols, and never imply that speculative details are proven.

## 29. Final definition
**ATLAS Portrait System = evidence-maximal reconstruction of the probable real human + one civilization-neutral visual language + explicit uncertainty + dignified historical monumentality.**

The system seeks **truthful reconstruction and historical glory simultaneously**. Glory is created by presentation; truth is never sacrificed to obtain it.


## 30. Yi Sun-sin pilot operational locks

The Yi Sun-sin pilot establishes the following production rules as canonical, not person-specific exceptions:

1. Do not generate first and research later. Complete the Research Dossier, evidence weighting and reconstruction decision before image generation.
2. Facial identity is the primary success criterion. If the face ceases to be recognizably the same person supported by the evidence, the portrait fails even if it is more beautiful.
3. Famous standard portraits or popular later images are never automatically canonical facial sources.
4. Armour is a high-risk AI invention surface. Shape, silhouette, colour layout, material, helmet form and ornament density must be evidence-locked before generation.
5. When person-specific clothing/armour evidence is incomplete, prefer a reconstruction produced, restored, exhibited or documented by a relevant national heritage body, national museum, public research institution or comparably authoritative specialist institution for the same period, region and status. Once adopted, AI must not redesign it for visual appeal.
6. A cleaner or more monumental result is not less historical merely because it is polished. Penalize polish only when it overwrites identity or material evidence.
7. Heroic presentation after accuracy is secured is an intended ATLAS rendering stage, not a defect.
8. The portrait asset should remain reusable and UI-independent; transparent background is the default project-delivery format unless a specific implementation constraint requires otherwise.

## 31. Mandatory five-stage production workflow

Every ATLAS portrait task follows this order:

1. **Research Dossier** — collect and organize appearance, clothing and contextual evidence.
2. **Evidence Assessment** — rank sources by directness, independence, provenance and reliability; record contradictions and uncertainty.
3. **Reconstruction Decision** — lock target age/period, facial traits, uncertain traits, clothing/armour basis, permitted inference and prohibited invention.
4. **Portrait Generation** — generate only from the locked reconstruction decision.
5. **Review / Limited Revision** — evaluate against the charter; revise only identified evidence, identity, realism, material-fidelity or project-asset defects.

A portrait generated without stages 1–3 is not canonical-ready.

## 32. Required per-person output package

Canonical completion requires four durable outputs:

### A. Research Dossier
The evidence gathered for the person, including relevant negative findings and unresolved questions.

### B. Evidence Assessment
The weighting of facial, clothing, armour, grooming and contextual sources, including provenance and uncertainty.

### C. Reconstruction Decision
The explicit decision describing what the portrait will depict, which traits are locked, what is inferred, what remains uncertain and what must not be invented.

### D. Final Portrait Asset
The reviewed image produced from the preceding three artifacts.

An image without the supporting research and reconstruction record is not a complete canonical portrait entry.

## 33. Final review order

Review in this exact priority:

1. **Historical/source accuracy**
2. **Facial identity and attested individual traits**
3. **Costume/armour/source fidelity**
4. **Living-human physical realism**
5. **Historical dignity, monumentality and glory**
6. **ATLAS common visual-language compatibility**
7. **Project insertion/reuse quality**

A lower-ranked virtue may not compensate for failure of a higher-ranked criterion.

## 34. Canonical new-chat bootstrap

Use the following instruction when starting a fresh portrait-production conversation:

> ATLAS 역사 초상 제작을 시작해.
>
> 먼저 `docs/ATLAS_HISTORICAL_PORTRAIT_CANONICAL_CHARTER.md`를 canonical 절대 기준으로 적용해.
>
> 바로 그림부터 만들지 말고 대상 인물의 **Research Dossier → Evidence Assessment → Reconstruction Decision**을 먼저 완료해.
>
> 얼굴은 실제 인간 복원을 최우선으로 하고, 직접 얼굴 자료와 확인 가능한 개인 특징을 최대한 보존해.
>
> 복식은 당사자 직접 자료가 있으면 그것을 따르고, 없으면 국가기관 또는 이에 준하는 권위 있는 기관의 시대·지역·신분 고증 자료를 기준으로 채택해. AI가 이를 다시 디자인하지 마.
>
> 판단 우선순위는 반드시 **고증·사료 정확성 > 개인 식별성과 확인된 특징 > 살아 있는 인간의 입체적 실재감 > 역사적 위엄·영광 > 미적 아름다움** 순서로 유지해.
>
> 정확성을 먼저 잠근 뒤 그 정확성 위에서 최대한 위대하게 표현해.
>
> 최종 표현은 **매우 사실적인 역사적 사실주의 + 신고전주의적 질서·품위·기념비성 + 절제된 낭만주의적 빛·깊이·영광**을 공통 화풍으로 사용해.
>
> 기본 구도는 **4:5 세로, 흉상, 자연스러운 약한 3/4 시점**으로 하고, 강한 직접자료가 다른 방향을 요구하면 동일인성을 우선해.
>
> 프로젝트 자산은 기본적으로 **투명 배경**으로 생성하고 이름·프레임·배지·시대 레이블·분야색 등 UI 요소는 이미지 안에 넣지 마.
>
> 생성 후에는 **사료 정확성 / 얼굴 동일인성 / 복식 자료 충실성 / 인간 실재감 / 역사적 위엄 / ATLAS 화풍 / 프로젝트 삽입성** 순서로 검수해.

## 35. Operational summary

> **ATLAS reconstructs the historical human, not the historical artwork.**
>
> **The face determines identity; clothing and armour prove the historical setting.**
>
> **AI may fill evidentiary gaps, but it may never overwrite evidence.**
>
> **Lock accuracy first; then express the person as greatly as that accuracy allows.**
