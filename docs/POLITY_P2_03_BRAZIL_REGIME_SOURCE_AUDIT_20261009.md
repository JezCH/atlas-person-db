## Evidence

Brazil's Decree No. 1 of **1889-11-15** proclaimed a *República Federativa* and established the *Estados Unidos do Brasil* by converting former imperial provinces into federal states. It explicitly removed monarchical government. The 1891 constitution affirmed the 1889 republican proclamation. Thus the Empire-to-Republic transition is a real constitutional rupture, although the provisional republican government preserved prior treaties and public obligations.

The 1967 constitution uses *República Federativa do Brasil* and continues the federal republic. Distinguish the later name/constitutional formulation from the original 1889 monarchical rupture; a new polity UUID solely for the later name is not automatically warranted.
