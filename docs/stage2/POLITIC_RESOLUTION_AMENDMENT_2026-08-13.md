# ATLAS Politic Resolution Amendment — 2026-08-13

## Status

Branch-only Stage 2 execution amendment. No Production mutation is authorized by this document.

This amendment preserves the earlier historical research files as evidence, but supersedes the execution assumption that every broad PeopleGroup, administrative context, event, or regime distinction must be replaced by a finer Person→Polity Activity.

## Resolution rule

ATLAS `Polity` is broader than a modern sovereign state. It exists partly so that pre-modern and transitional political contexts can be represented without forcing modern state terminology onto city-states, tribal polities, confederacies, quasi-independent domains, warlord authorities, or similar political actors.

The operational rule is therefore:

1. If a historically clear state adequately describes the person's political context, use that state.
2. If no clear state adequately describes it, use a source-backed political actor at the necessary resolution.
3. Create a finer political actor only when it exercised meaningful independent/quasi-independent authority or when a real allegiance/power change would be erased by the broader context.
4. Do not manufacture a micro-Polity solely to avoid using a broad tribal/people label. PeopleGroup identity and Polity identity remain separate even if an explicitly reviewed Polity uses the same conventional label.
5. Government/regime, event, administrative region, PeopleGroup, role and chronology metadata may add precision without forcing Person Activity fragmentation.
6. `rules`, `governs`, `serves`, `active_in`, `opposes`, `claims_rule` control the meaning of the Person→Polity link. In particular, `active_in` and `serves` must never be rendered as personal territorial rule.
7. Person never owns Geometry. Territory remains Polity + time → Territory History → Geometry.

The machine-readable contract is `stage2/contracts/politic-resolution-current.v1.json`.

## Why the previous frontier changed

The earlier P5/P6 preparation was intentionally conservative and separated People/Event/Governance aggressively. That was useful for finding category mistakes, but it overgeneralized those boundaries into a rule that required finer political actors even when the existing broad politic was fit for ATLAS's purpose.

The old `57 / 24 / 3` frontier is retained as research/audit evidence. The effective execution frontier is now generated by applying `stage2/integration/baseline-a-politic-resolution-amendments.v1.json` on top of it.

## Re-evaluation of the former remaining 10

### Removed from the P6 structural-correction frontier

- **Pocatello** — keep `Northwestern Shoshone` as the reviewed coarse tribal politic; use `active_in` rather than inventing a Pocatello-specific micro-Polity or implying rule over all Northwestern Shoshone.
- **Sitting Bull** — keep `Lakota` as the coarse politic; use relation/role/chronology to represent leadership without creating an artificial autonomous-following Polity.
- **Niccolo Machiavelli** — keep the stable Florence political identity. Republic/Medici government changes do not by themselves require a Person-politic split. Government/public-office chronology can be modeled separately.

These rows may still receive ordinary relation/name/metadata backfill later, but they are not structural Correction v2 blockers.

### Still require P6 correction

- **Poundmaker** — keep `Cree`; do not split into Red Pheasant and Poundmaker-band Polities. The legacy leadership start/role still needs source-backed chronology/role correction.
- **Shi Xie** — `Jiaozhou` is not the politic target. Split because there is a real political-allegiance change: Eastern Han before the 210 transition and the stable Sun/Sun Quan/Wu political actor after it. Governance/Region authoring is optional metadata, not a prerequisite.
- **William I of Orange** — retire `Dutch Revolt` as a Polity. Replace the two separate Holland/Zeeland Person-politic targets with one source-backed transitional Dutch rebel-provinces political actor from the governing phase, plus a separate `opposes` relation to the Spanish Monarchy. The Dutch Revolt may remain an Event independently.
- **Fang Guozhen** — remains a genuine state-breakdown/warlord case. Use one continuous eastern-Zhejiang regional Polity for the Person Activity; changing Yuan/other superior relationships belong in time-bounded Polity→Polity relations rather than repeated Person fragmentation.
- **Vercingetorix** — `Gaul` is too broad/geographic. Relink to the reviewed `Arverni` political actor. Wider Gallic-coalition Event metadata is optional and does not block the relink.
- **Leftraru** — keep `Mapuche` as the coarse politic and correct the invalid leadership chronology. Do not require a synthetic wartime-coalition Polity. People/Event metadata can coexist separately.
- **Jesus** — an exact 27–30 `Roman Empire` Activity already exists for the same Person/role/period and explicitly represents the public ministry at map-level polity resolution. Retire the over-specific `Roman Judaea` row rather than creating a Galilee Tetrarchy Person-politic row.
