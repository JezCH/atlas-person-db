## 3. Legal and political-historical evidence; where official date sets diverge

- [French Interior Ministry official civic history](https://formation-civique.interieur.gouv.fr/fiches-par-thematiques/histoire-geographie-et-culture/les-regimes-politiques-depuis-1789/les-regimes-politiques/) calls pre-1789 France a monarchy, then catalogues several Republic, Consulate, Empire, Restoration and constitutional eras after 1789. **Country name 'France' cannot be given Republican constitutional legitimacy retroactively across the medieval Kingdom**. The modern French Republic is one *form of government* of France, not another independent country merely because two normalized records exist.
- [French National Assembly historical regimes table](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/gouv_parl/regimes.asp) lists successive First/Second/Third Republic and monarchic/imperial systems; it uses **administrative regimes-of-government dating conventions**: specifically Third 1870-09-04→1940-07-10, wartime 1940-07-10→1944-08-25, GPRF 1944-08-25→1947-01-16, Fourth 1947-01-16→1959-01-08, Fifth from 1959-01-08. These are **not identical** to separate legal-government establishment or *constitutional promulgation/entry-into-force* records: P2-01D/E registers the actual 1944-06-03 formation of GPRF and the 1946-12-24 effectiveness of the 1946 Constitution, and Fifth 1958-10-04 promulgation; those are distinct event- and regime-dating axes. **Do not silently 'correct' one authoritative periodization using another** without explicitly defining the represented date type.
- [9 August 1944 official Légifrance restoration ordinance, Article 1](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006530175) affirms the Republic's juridical continuity, while [Article 7](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006530181) identifies Vichy as a **de facto** `État français` government. These govern legality as viewed by the restoring French republican authority. They **neither deny that Vichy actually administered territory and persecuted people nor make Vichy another legitimate Republican cabinet**.
- The temporal Third Republic `state_form` and CNF/CFLN/GPRF/Fourth/Fifth period Contexts are appropriately attached to the Republic UUID because they refer to specific constitutional/political authority. The medieval kingship and country-level literary activities on the France umbrella are separately reviewable facts. Overlapping year ranges alone do **not** determine identical Person Activity facts or a parent-child Polity graph.
